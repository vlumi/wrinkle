## Findings

1. **The fan-out worker is the bottleneck, not the gateway.** The send round trip stays at 18 to 19 ms up to 1000/s, so storing a message is cheap. The worker handles records one at a time, with two transactions of about five round trips each per message, and tops out somewhere between 100/s and 500/s. Fix: handle partitions and recipients concurrently, and drop the idempotency lookup in favor of the unique key.
2. **The protocol leaked backlog between runs.** The consumer group's offsets survive the backend restart, so each run started by draining what the previous one left in Kafka; at 1000/s and 2000/s the worker never reached the run's own messages. Fix: reset the group's offsets to the end of the topic before every run.
3. **The latency floor is the change-data path.** At 100/s, where nothing queues, end to end is 441 ms against a 19 ms send; Debezium polls every 500 ms by default. Worth lowering in the shared connector config so backend differences are visible.

The CPU-per-message figures at 500/s and above include time spent on the previous run's backlog and are not comparable.
